# UAE Visa Page Architecture — 6-Week Expansion Plan

## Why this plan exists

Your current `/uae-visa` page is comprehensive (1,002 lines, 11 visa types, 21 FAQs, multi-schema). But search behaviour shows people search for **specific visa durations** — `"30 days multiple entry uae visa"`, `"60 days a2a visa extension"`, `"96 hour transit visa"` — and Google overwhelmingly rewards pages with URLs that match those exact phrases.

**uaevisaonline.com has 16 separate visa-type URLs.** That's not bloat — that's why they rank. Each URL is a separate "best match" for a specific long-tail query, and they internal-link all 16 into the hub at `/`.

This plan builds 8 high-priority sister pages that orbit `/uae-visa`, capturing the long-tail traffic that the hub page alone can't.

## Sprint sequence — week by week

### Week 1 — A2A extension pages (the unique cluster competitors own)

**Why these first:** A2A is the *biggest keyword gap* you have. Your competitors (uaevisaonline + akbartravels) both have dedicated A2A pages; you have none. The search intent is high-urgency (people search this when their visa is about to expire), conversion rate is excellent, and there's almost zero brand-named UAE travel agency competing for it.

**Page 1: `/uae-a2a-visa-extension-30-days`**

- H1: "UAE A2A Visa Extension 30 Days — Inside-Country Visa Extension Without Leaving"
- Title tag: "UAE A2A Visa Extension 30 Days 2026 | From AED 550 | 2-3 Day Processing"
- Meta: "Extend your UAE visa 30 days from inside the country — no exit needed. A2A (Apply-To-Apply) inside-country extension from AED 550. Processing 2-3 days."
- Content blocks: Eligibility (who can use it), exact process (5 steps), required documents, cost breakdown, 8 FAQs, comparison table (A2A vs border run), 4 testimonials.
- Schema: Service + FAQPage + BreadcrumbList + Offer with price AED 550.
- Internal links FROM: `/uae-visa` hub (the A2A section), 30-day tourist page (eventually), homepage footer.
- Internal links TO: `/uae-visa`, `/uae-a2a-visa-extension-60-days`, FAQ on visa rules.
- Word count target: 1,200–1,600 words.

**Page 2: `/uae-a2a-visa-extension-60-days`**

- Same structure as #1 with 60-day specifics.
- Title: "UAE A2A Visa Extension 60 Days 2026 | From AED 850 | Inside-Country Extension"
- Cross-link tightly with the 30-day page (canonical sibling).
- Mention 90-day extension as a callout but defer building that page until volume justifies it.

### Week 2 — Transit visa pages (high commercial intent)

**Why these second:** Transit visa queries have *immediate* commercial intent — someone with a 12+ hour layover deciding whether to step out of the airport. The 96-hour version has higher volume than 48-hour because the price-vs-effort math is more favorable for travelers.

**Page 3: `/uae-96-hour-transit-visa`**

## SEO mechanics to apply to every page

Every one of the 8 pages must include:

1. **`TouristTrip` OR `Service` schema** with concrete `Offer` price.
2. **`FAQPage` schema** with 5–8 questions (Google loves these for the rich snippet).
3. **`BreadcrumbList` schema** showing `Home → UAE Visa → [Specific Visa Type]`.
4. **A canonical link** pointing to the page itself (not the hub).
5. **Title format:** `[Specific Phrase] 2026 | From AED [Price] | [Time-to-Process]` — the year and price are CTR magnets.
6. **One H1, structured H2s, named #anchor IDs** for every section.
7. **Width/height on every `<img>`** so CLS stays at zero.
8. **Internal link to `/uae-visa`** in the first paragraph (anchor text: "complete UAE visa guide" or similar).
9. **CTA above the fold** (WhatsApp button + inline enquiry form).
10. **Word count 1,000–1,800** — long enough for ranking, short enough to read.

## Risks and how to mitigate them

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Thin/duplicate content (Google might see siblings as near-duplicates) | Each page has unique opening section, unique FAQs, unique testimonials. Use different word patterns to describe the same processes. |
| Cannibalization with the `/uae-visa` hub | Hub stays comprehensive but high-level. Each sibling goes deep on one visa type. Internal linking signals hierarchy. |
| Pages buried with no inbound links | Hub page gets a prominent "Specific visa types" grid linking to all 8 siblings. Footer adds same links. Blog posts cross-reference. |
| Google takes 8 weeks to re-index | Submit each URL manually via GSC URL Inspection. Often re-indexes in 3–7 days. |
| You miss factual updates (e.g., govt changes price) | Include a `Last updated: [date]` stamp at the top of every page. Set a calendar reminder to review prices quarterly. |
